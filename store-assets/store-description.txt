Porcelain Botany Theme presses a botanical study onto porcelain for VS Code: cream-white paper and warm sand surfaces in the light variant, deep slate in the dark one, with dusty mauve, sage green and blue-gray slate as accents. Two coordinated variants ship in one extension and are cut from a single palette, so switching between them changes the light level rather than the character of the workspace. Syntax colors come from the same garden: dusty-mauve keywords, storage and tags, sage strings, blue-gray slate functions, and amber for numbers, constants and attributes, each checked against its own background. Semantic highlighting, the full 16-color terminal ANSI palette, Git decorations, find and selection colors, list states, buttons, badges and diff editor backgrounds are all themed.
