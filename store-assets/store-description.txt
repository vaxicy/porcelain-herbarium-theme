Porcelain Herbarium Theme presses a botanical study onto porcelain for VS Code: cream-white paper and warm sand surfaces in the light variant, deep slate in the dark one, with dusty mauve, sage green and blue-gray slate as accents. Two coordinated variants ship in one extension and are cut from a single palette, so switching between them changes the light level rather than the character of the workspace. Syntax colors come from the same garden: dusty-mauve keywords, storage and tags, sage strings, blue-gray slate functions, and amber for numbers, constants and attributes, each checked against its own background. Semantic highlighting, the full 16-color terminal ANSI palette, Git decorations, find and selection colors, list states, buttons, badges and diff editor backgrounds are all themed. Porcelain Herbarium Theme 把一份植物标本压在瓷面上：浅色版是瓷白的纸与暖砂的底色，深色版沉成深石板，再用干花藕紫、鼠尾草绿和灰蓝点缀。同一个扩展里带着两套变体，共用一套色板，切换时只是明暗变了，配色还是同一套。语法配色也长在同一座花园里：关键字、storage 和标签是藕紫，字符串是鼠尾草绿，函数是灰蓝，数字、常量与属性留琥珀色，两套底色上都逐一校过对比度。语义高亮、终端完整 16 色 ANSI、Git 装饰色、查找与选中色、列表状态、按钮、角标、差异编辑器底色也都配好了。基于非商业使用许可发布，个人、教育及非商业用途均可用。
